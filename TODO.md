### Making the trail solid

From the deep review of 2026-10-21 (150 agents over Kofi's week of 14–21 October; the report stays in that session's scratchpad). The finished day mostly looks right; the jank is motion. The trail changes on its own while you watch, and the marker, the card and the trail disagree with each other. Root causes: the map works the trail out again from a window that slides every 15 s and names its results by that window; rules that need a long memory read a history whose length follows the Trail setting; "is he home" is decided six ways and "is this fix real" four; and rules patched one sighting at a time sit on knife-edge thresholds. From now on, every change to the trail must keep or improve the replay's score (step 0), on both weeks once there are two. Adam's answers (2026-10-22): Kofi does go under the bridge and into the courtyards now and then, rarely; trips of 2–5 minutes to the pee spot east at the park's edge count as outings (on the timeline, in front on the map, "left Home"); the Home place is in the right place; he was out on 21/10 at 07:28 (not an error); 16/10 14:53 and 21/10 after 15:40 unknown; a second week comes on Sunday 25 October.

How, from 2026-10-22: fast where it's safe, careful where it isn't. Each step has a risk: **high** (where the asset is, or the line it walked: what people trust the map for), **medium**, or **low** (drawing, tidying). High: the replay no worse on both weeks, one full review (finders, then two skeptics per finding), the fixes, then one check of the fixes (skeptics only for findings of medium or more); no third round: what's low by then goes here unless it's a one-liner. Medium: the replay gate, one review with one skeptic per finding, the fixes. Low: the tests and the replay gate (and a look in the browser for drawing), no review workflow. A release per high step; medium and low ones together. Effort (Adam, 2026-10-22): high for 1b, 4, 5 and 6; medium for the rest (3's finish, 7, 8, 9, 10), set in the app for this session, at start for sessions of their own, and per agent in review workflows.
